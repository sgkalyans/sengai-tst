What does Sengai do?

Sengai arranges freshly prepared, home-style meals for elderly parents and loved ones in Pattukkottai, delivered to their home. It is for families who live away from them — abroad in places like Singapore, Dubai, the UK or the USA, or in another Indian city such as Chennai or Bengaluru.

Which areas do you deliver to?

Pattukkottai town and the surrounding areas in Thanjavur district, Tamil Nadu. Share your parents’ address in the form and we’ll confirm that we can reach them.

I’m an NRI living abroad. How do I arrange meals for my parents?

Fill in the form on this page from anywhere, in any time zone. Our team will contact you on WhatsApp, in English or Tamil, to understand your parents’ needs and set up their meals. Your parents only deal with our local team.

Can you deliver tiffin, lunch or dinner every day?

Meals can be arranged daily, weekly or only on the days they need. Tell us which meals your parents would like — morning tiffin, lunch or dinner — and we’ll confirm what we can arrange for them.

Can the food suit my parents’ taste and diet?

Every meal is arranged around the person eating it, with a familiar, home-style taste. Tell us their preferences — vegetarian, less spice, less salt or foods they avoid — and we’ll talk through what we can do.

Do my parents need a smartphone or an app?

No. Our local team delivers the meals to their door. You arrange everything with us, with one point of contact on WhatsApp.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the meals and schedule you choose. Leave your details in the form and we’ll share the details with you on WhatsApp.

Do you help with anything other than meals?

Meals come first. Grocery help, medicine collection, doctor visit support and other everyday help are coming soon, but are not available yet. Tell us in the form which would help your family most.
